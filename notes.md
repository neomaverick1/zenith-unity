Zenith, built in Unity 6: a 2048 merge puzzle where every tile is a star, with a soundtrack that grows as you play.

**Android:** install `Zenith.apk`. It's a separate app from the web Zenith (`com.neomaverick.zenithunity`), so both can be on one phone.

**Google Play:** `Zenith-Play.aab` is the bundle for the Play Console. It's signed with the demo key; sign it with the Play upload key before uploading.

**iPhone:** `Zenith-iOS-Xcode.zip` is the Xcode project. Open `Unity-iPhone.xcodeproj` on a Mac, choose your team under Signing & Capabilities, and run.

**Web:** play it at https://neomaverick1.github.io/zenith-unity/

What's inside: custom tile, board and nebula shaders with HDR bloom, particle bursts, lens punches on huge merges, six synchronized music intensities that build on the downbeat, merge bells tuned to the chord, native haptics on Android and iPhone, three modes, boards from 3×3 to 6×6, records and twelve achievements.
